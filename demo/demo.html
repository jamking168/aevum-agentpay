AgentPay

Session-scoped spending controls for an AI agent paying merchants on Aevum.
five limits, all enforced on chain
budget        total the agent may ever spend
perPaymentCap largest single invoice
expiry        session dies on its own
revoke        payer can stop it instantly
idempotency   one payment per order id
